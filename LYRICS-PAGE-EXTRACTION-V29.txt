VerseFlow V29 - Lyrics Page Extraction

- Search box accepts song title, artist, or both. The word "find" is not required.
- USE / EDIT now fetches a selected normal song webpage and extracts its dedicated Lyrics section.
- Crawl4AI returns raw markdown when available and allows a larger page payload so lyrics located lower on long pages are not truncated.
- Jina Reader fallback also keeps a larger safe read-only page payload.
- Streaming/redirect pages remain metadata-only because they normally contain no lyric section.
- Existing .git and .env files are not modified.
