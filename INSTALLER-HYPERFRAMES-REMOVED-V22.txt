VERSEFLOW V22 - HYPERFRAMES REMOVED

HyperFrames is no longer required, checked, installed, exposed through Electron IPC, or shown in Production Control.
The one-file installer remains INSTALL_VERSEFLOW.bat and now checks 11 tool groups instead of 12.
Existing HyperFrames installations on the computer are left alone; VerseFlow does not uninstall third-party software.
